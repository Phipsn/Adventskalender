Frequently asked questions

General

How do I take part?

Send a WhatsApp message to +49 1523 4796360 or an email to matsundroxy@gmail.com and pay the entry fee of €5.

How does it work?

From the 1st Sunday of Advent (intro) you receive a short story every day via WhatsApp broadcast, plus a riddle every Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday.

What do I need to take part?

A phone with WhatsApp and internet access.

How do I answer the riddles?

You answer by WhatsApp message. Each riddle comes with exact instructions. Please only send your answers and no other messages while the riddles are running. Your first answer counts, and a sent answer must not be deleted or edited!

How is the winner determined?

The person who solves the riddle fastest on the 4th Sunday of Advent wins. To do so, all previous riddles must have been solved. If nobody solves the last riddle, the person who has solved the most riddles correctly by the end of the 4th Sunday of Advent wins. If there is still a tie (several people with the same number of correctly solved riddles), the winner is decided by a "tie-break riddle" on 24 December. The participants concerned will be informed in advance.

What happens to the money?

All entry fees are used entirely for the prizes and a donation to charity. How the money is used is disclosed openly: the donations go to Kinderschutz-Zentrum Berlin e.V., Platane 19 e.V. and Welthungerhilfe. Regardless of the number of participants, I guarantee a minimum donation of €100 in total. Any remaining amount after the cost of the prizes is also donated to these three organisations.

What happens to my data?

Your number is treated confidentially and is not shared in a WhatsApp group. Riddles are sent via broadcast, and no data is passed on to third parties.

Why do we do this?

For the fun of riddles, a passion for writing and the joy of Advent calendars!

Is this illegal gambling or a lottery?

No. Under German law, prize puzzles based on mental skill are not games of chance, since they do not depend on luck. Legal references (German): Preisrätsel mit Denksportaufgaben unterfallen mangels Abhängigkeit vom Zufall nicht dem § 287 StGB (nachzulesen in: Schönke/Schröder/Heine/Hecker StGB § 287 Rn.5 und § 284 Rn. 7), außerdem: BGH, Urt. v. 8.8.2017 − 1 StR 519/16 (LG Chemnitz). Bei dem Rätsel Adventskalender handelt es sich nicht um ein Glücksspiel nach dem GlüStV (siehe hierzu insbesondere BVerwG, Urteil vom 22.1.2014 – 8 C 26/12).

What are the prizes?

The fastest person on the 4th Sunday of Advent wins a gold coin (1 g). Second place wins a silver coin (1/2 oz), third place a real ancient bronze coin.

Which good cause is supported?

By taking part you also support a good cause. The donation goes to Kinderschutz-Zentrum Berlin e.V., Platane 19 e.V. and Welthungerhilfe.

Riddles and schedule

What kind of riddles are they?

The riddles are our own creations and are aimed at teenagers and adults. They need no special knowledge, just clever deduction and research (using the internet is allowed!). Wrapped in charming short stories by AIfred, the riddles contain clear tasks as well as subtle hints between the lines. They are usually neither trivia questions nor classic brain-teasers or maths problems.

What kind of stories are they?

The riddles come with short stories by AIfred, about 2 A4 pages each.

How long do I have to answer?

There are no deadlines this year! The fastest riddle solver on the 4th Sunday of Advent wins. Solving the earlier riddles before then will certainly be a decisive advantage, though.

What if I could not solve a riddle?

You can of course keep taking part and solving riddles. The last riddle builds on the previous ones, so it may not be solvable if earlier riddles were not solved correctly.

How are the riddles published?

The riddles are sent to everyone at the same time via WhatsApp. Speed only counts for the last riddle. There are no deadlines!
